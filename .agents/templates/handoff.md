# Handoff Template

> Use when session is ending with unfinished items, or work needs to be handed off to another session.

```markdown
## Handoff — [date] — [task name]

### Objective
[What this session was trying to accomplish]

### Completed
- [What was completed]

### Pending
- [What was left and why]

### Affected Files
- [file_path] — [change summary]

### Validation Steps
[How to confirm the completed work is correct]

### Risks & Watchpoints
- [Risk 1] — [what to watch for]

### Next Step
[Actionable starting point: open X, find function Y, add Z]
```
